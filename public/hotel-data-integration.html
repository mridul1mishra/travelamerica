Hotel tier scoring model

The scoring model replaces the earlier qualitative proposals as the basis for tier decisions. No hotel has been assigned a score automatically. All 50 await evidence-backed scoring; existing database tiers remain unchanged.

Weights and calculation

Total = Σ (dimension score ÷ 4 × weight). Each dimension is scored 0–4. Unknown is a separate value, never zero. All five dimensions must have a score, an evidence note and an official source before this scorecard produces a tier.

DimensionWeight
Rooms and bathrooms30%
Guest service30%
Dining15%
Wellness and facilities15%
Design and finish10%

Standard: below 50. Premium: 50 to below 75. Luxury: 75–100, with rooms and guest service each scoring at least 3/4. A score of 75 or more that fails either Luxury requirement produces Premium. Thresholds use the unrounded total.

Assess the regular property-wide offering. Suite-only or special-floor benefits do not upgrade the whole hotel. Separately listed hotel-within-a-hotel inventory may be assessed separately.

This is an editorial model, not an industry certification. Ratings, price, brand and marketing language are not scoring substitutes. The rubric is an operational draft for pilot review; it improves consistency but still needs documented judgment. A facility being unmentioned does not prove it is absent.
